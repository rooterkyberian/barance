★★★

Load the bar to hit the goal weight — the total is what counts, and the bar has to stay up. Click plates to pick them up — keep clicking to stack more in hand (up to 20 kg; a lone 25 always fits). Click a sleeve to slide the stack on, click the floor to set it down, right-click or Esc to put everything back. Only the outermost plate comes off — any plate further in can still be dragged along the sleeve, shoving whatever it meets. Plates in hand are weightless — but if the bar tips past 15° it’s gone, and the plates will ride the sleeve on the way down.
